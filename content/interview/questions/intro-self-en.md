## Structure
- **Present**: current role and what you own.
- **Past**: one achievement that proves it, with a number.
- **Future**: why this role is the logical next step.

## Useful phrases
- "Most recently, I led…"
- "What I enjoy most is…"
- "I'm looking for a team where…"
